## Geometry and limitations

Initial diamond T=(0,-1),R=(1,0),B=(0,1),L=(-1,0). Upper side creases x-0.5y=0.5 and -x-0.5y=0.5 map side tips to (+/-0.2,0.4), leaving a central gap. After turnover, side creases x=+/-0.4 map side points (+/-2/3,1/3) to (+/-2/15,1/3). Their lower endpoints lie at y=0.6. Bottom fold y=0.6 maps B to (0,0.2). Left half closes behind the right with a mountain fold, retaining the pale lower belly outside. The chosen head hinge x+y=-0.65 intersects spine (0,-0.65) and upper outer edge (7/60,-23/30), mapping T to (0.35,-0.65).

Flat layers are clipped/reflected with face changes; mountain closure places moved layers beneath the fixed half. All14 states retain paper-layer area2. The head cap is a projected closed result plus an explicitly schematic opening inset: it is NOT a validated 3D outside-wrap/layer-access simulation. The outside reverse must wrap OVER the neck exterior, not inside it. Physical testing must verify this and standing at the chosen proportions.
