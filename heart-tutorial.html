Origami Heart - Step-by-Step Tutorial

Fold a flat heart with 11 illustrated steps, from the centre creases to the rounded lobes.

Jump to instructionsPrint / save as PDF

Materials

  • One square sheet of paper
  • A flat folding surface

No scissors or glue needed. New to folding? Read the beginner’s guide.

How to read these diagrams

Each picture shows BEFORE on the left and AFTER on the right at the same scale. Pink is the coloured face; white is the reverse. Blue dashed lines mark active folds; arrows show moving corners. Grey dotted lines are existing creases. Steps 2 and 3 end reopened; steps 8 and 11 turn the whole model over. Outlines show ideal layer boundaries, not paper thickness. Read the words together with each picture.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Set the starting orientation

    Use a two-colour square about 15 cm along each side. Put the white side up with a corner at the top, like a diamond. The opposite corner points toward you. Keep this orientation unless told to turn over.

    Open step 1 diagram for zooming

  2. Make the vertical centre crease

    Bring the left corner onto the right corner. Match the edges, crease, then reopen completely. The crease runs from top corner to bottom corner.

    Open step 2 diagram for zooming

  3. Make the horizontal centre crease

    Bring the bottom corner onto the top corner. Crease, then reopen completely. The creases cross at the centre. Your paper is a flat white diamond again.

    Open step 3 diagram for zooming

  4. Bring the top tip to the centre

    Fold the top corner down to the crease crossing. Keep this flap folded. The NEW top edge is horizontal, halfway between the old top corner and the centre. The folded triangle shows the coloured side.

    Open step 4 diagram for zooming

  5. Bring the bottom tip to the new top edge

    Lift the bottom corner to the midpoint of the NEW horizontal top edge made in step 4, on the vertical centre crease. Do not aim for the original top corner or the centre crossing. Press the new horizontal fold. Check for a straight lower edge and a point at each side.

    Open step 5 diagram for zooming

  6. Lift the right half

    Keep the straight lower edge nearest you. Hold its midpoint still. Lift the lower-right section with every layer beneath it up and inward so the RIGHT HALF of the lower edge lies on the vertical centreline. Its outer end meets the midpoint of the top edge. Make the diagonal crease from the lower midpoint toward the upper-right sloping edge. Do not rotate the whole model.

    Open step 6 diagram for zooming

  7. Lift the left half

    Repeat with the lower-left section and every layer beneath it. Align the LEFT HALF of the lower edge on the same centreline, beside the edge folded in step 6. Keep the right lobe in place. Check for two equally high upper tips, a notch between them and one bottom point. A centre seam is normal.

    Open step 7 diagram for zooming

  8. Turn the model over

    Turn the whole model over from left to right, like turning a page. Keep the bottom point down. You now see the back, where the shaping flaps will remain. This is a turn-over, not a rotation on the table.

    Open step 8 diagram for zooming

  9. Fold the side tips inward

    On the back, fold the far-right point inward toward the centre; repeat at the far-left point. Move all layers at each small tip. Keep both new creases vertical and equal. In these diagrams each tip moves about two-fifths of its horizontal distance to the centreline. These are shaping folds: compare both sides rather than forcing a thick stack.

    Open step 9 diagram for zooming

  10. Fold both top tips down

    Still on the back, fold each upper tip down to the height of the centre notch. Keep the new creases horizontal and at the same height. Fold only the little pointed caps, not the whole lobes. Press gently and keep the notch open.

    Open step 10 diagram for zooming

  11. Turn to the front and check

    Turn the whole model over left to right again, keeping the bottom point down. The shaping flaps should be behind the coloured front. Check for equal lobes, an open notch and a downward point. If a fold does not match the diagram, return to the last matching step.

    Open step 11 diagram for zooming

Helpful folding tips

  • Line up corners before pressing a crease from the middle outwards.
  • Keep the paper facing the same way as the instruction before each fold.

If a fold goes wrong

  • If layers do not line up, return to the last clear step rather than forcing the next fold.
  • Thick paper becomes difficult to fold as layers build up; try a larger, thinner square.
