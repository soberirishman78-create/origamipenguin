Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

OrigamiPenguin.com is a simple content website. We aim to collect as little information about you as possible. This page explains what does and doesn't happen with your data when you visit.

What We Don't Do

This site has no user accounts, no comment system, no newsletter, and no contact forms. We do not ask for, store, or sell any personal information. We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Analytics

We may use privacy-focused, cookie-free analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic — for example, how many people visit and which tutorials are most popular. This kind of analytics does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not track you across other websites.

Hosting and Technical Logs

This site is served through Cloudflare and its underlying hosting provider. Like nearly all web infrastructure, these services may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) for security and performance purposes. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details on their processing.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site go to Amazon.com and include our affiliate tag, which tells Amazon the visit came from this site. Once you're on Amazon, their own privacy policy applies. See our full affiliate disclosure for how these links work.

Children's Privacy

Our tutorials are family-friendly, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we add functionality that changes how data is handled (for example, a newsletter or contact form), we will update this page before it goes live.
