Materials

  • One square sheet of paper
  • A flat folding surface

No scissors or glue needed. New to folding? Read the beginner’s guide.

Existing steps — unverified

  1. Fold and Unfold Diagonally

    Fold your square paper in half diagonally, then unfold. Repeat along the other diagonal so both creases are visible.

  2. Fold in Half Both Ways

    Fold the paper in half horizontally and unfold, then vertically and unfold. You should now have four creases crossing in the center.

  3. Collapse into a Square Base

    Using the creases as guides, bring the corners together and collapse the paper into a smaller square shape.

  4. Fold Up the Sides

    Fold the left and right edges of the top layer inward to the center line, forming a kite shape. Repeat on the back.

  5. Petal Fold the Wings

    Lift each flap upward and flatten it into a petal fold to form the crane's wings on both sides.

  6. Shape the Head and Tail

    Fold one narrow point down slightly to create the head using a reverse fold, and leave the other as the tail.

  7. Your Crane is Complete!

    Gently pull the wings apart and curve them for a finished look. Add a fold to the head for a beak if you'd like.

Helpful folding tips

  • Line up corners before pressing a crease from the middle outwards.
  • Keep the paper facing the same way as the instruction before each fold.

If a fold goes wrong

  • If layers do not line up, return to the last clear step rather than forcing the next fold.
  • Thick paper becomes difficult to fold as layers build up; try a larger, thinner square.
