E-Reader

Open a book or document from your device. Files stay in your browser.

Open separately

Drop a file anywhere in this reading window.

Choose a document to start reading.

Text replacement uses white-backed text. Pages with replacements are saved as images, so their text will no longer be selectable. Scanned pages can use Add text and Highlight.

Supported files EPUB, PDF, Word (.docx), HTML, plain text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, and common images up to 80 MB. EPUBs have page turns and chapter navigation. Word and HTML use a simplified reading view. Markdown and CSV open as text. PDF controls depend on your browser. Use Edit PDF to replace visible text, add notes, or highlight a page and save a separate copy. The editor loads its tools from jsDelivr, and the document stays on your device. DRM-protected books and older .doc files are unsupported.
